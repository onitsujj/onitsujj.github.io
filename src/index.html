01 About

I'm the person you put on the requirement nobody can pin down.

Most of my work starts where the spec runs out — the requirement everyone agrees matters and no one can quite describe.

For ten years I've built high-performance Java systems for enterprise: web platforms, data integrations, the unglamorous plumbing that has to be right. For the last five I've led the teams that build them — owning the plan, putting the right people on the right problems, and keeping quality non-negotiable when timelines aren't.

What I care about is judgment: choosing the technology a problem actually needs, not the one that's familiar. That's also why AI adoption is the work I lead now. Used well, it changes how a team designs, reviews, and decides. Used carelessly, it just adds noise — I help teams tell the difference.

02 Approach

Adopting AI is a shift in how a team thinks — not a tool you roll out.

01

Discipline first. AI should sharpen how a team already designs and reviews — not replace the judgment that made it good.

02

Ship, then trust. I'd rather show one system working than promise ten. Adoption follows proof, not slides.

03

The right tool, honestly. Sometimes the answer is AI. Sometimes it's a boring, reliable pattern. Knowing which is the whole job.
