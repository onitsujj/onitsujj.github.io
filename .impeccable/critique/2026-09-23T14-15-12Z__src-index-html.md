## Design Specificity Verdict
Mostly authored. The lanyard badge with the real Mandrill strap, the pinned "magnifies" keynote, and real stage photos belong only to this person. The skeleton around them (eyebrow, big H1, 5/7 About split, row list, centered CTA) is the stock dark-portfolio template. Missed: the talk photos (strongest proof) sit greyscale at the bottom; the "requirement nobody can pin down" line never gets a real example; Connect ends on a hedge.

Detector: CLI 22 findings (src), 2 on index.html alone; browser overlay 23 desktop / 16 mobile. Real: tight-leading on .belief__text (1.25-1.28), line-length ~94ch on .row-card__desc (desktop), off-scale font sizes 11px/16px/24px. False positives: ai-color-palette x10 (the documented Spotlight Cyan), eyebrow/kicker/all-caps (documented wayfinding language), badge clasp colors/radii (skeuomorphic material), clipped-overflow on ScrollSmoother/pin wrappers, flat-type-hierarchy (static scan can't resolve /css/main.css), lightbox broken-image (src set at open), skip-link black (static read; CSS sets #04222a).

## Overall Impression
Strong hero, strong thesis, honest fallbacks. The page loses momentum in the middle (long pin) and ends weak (small Talks heading, grey photos, a hedging CTA line). Biggest opportunity: make the LinkedIn follow one tap from anywhere, and let the stage photos carry the proof.

## What's Working
1. One Spotlight discipline: cyan marks only what to read or do next.
2. The badge as signature object, with a pixel-matched static twin for mobile and no-JS.
3. Honest fallbacks: reduced-motion three-column Approach, reveal failsafe, skip link, focus ring.

## Priority Issues
- **[P1] The conversion takes two steps, and on mobile it is buried.** Nav "Follow" and hero "Let's connect" both scroll to #connect (index.html:93, 153). On mobile, Follow lives only in the hamburger drawer and the first screen is mostly badge. Fix: nav Follow links straight to LinkedIn; keep a compact Follow visible in the mobile bar; shrink the mobile badge so the H1 is above the fold. Command: clarify + adapt.
- **[P1] Progress bar misreports position.** Fills fully at ~50% of the page. Fix: refresh the trigger after pin spacing exists, or drive it from ScrollSmoother's real max scroll. Command: harden.
- **[P1] Approach pin is too long for its content.** ~4.5 screens for four short paragraphs, no skip; mid-scrub frames are grey and off-center. Fix: cut to ~2.5 screens, end on all four beliefs as a recap, drop the redundant keys. Command: distill + animate.
- **[P2] The proof is under-weighted.** Talks h2 unsized; photos greyscale at rest; strip cut at container edge with no drag cue; one lonely "5" stat. Fix: full h2 size, color photos (or one large packed-hall photo), a visible drag/count cue, a second real stat or a sentence. Command: bolder + layout.
- **[P2] Collisions and polish bugs.** Section-index label overlaps its active tick; mobile SCROLL cue sits on the CTAs; 3D strap glow washes over the "About" nav link; .hero__sub at 1.7 line-height breaks "full-/time"; active underline shows under the filled Follow button; belief text leading 1.25-1.28; talk descriptions ~94ch. Command: polish.
